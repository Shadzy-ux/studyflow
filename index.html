Rules to finish on time

  • Tick a task only after you can explain it without looking at notes.
  • For LIC and TLRF, solve at least 2–3 numericals/design problems per study block.
  • For IoT, make tangible progress: diagram, code, wiring, dashboard, testing or documentation.
  • On 30–31 October, prioritise timed question-paper practice and weak topics instead of rereading everything.
  • If you miss a block, move it to the next day’s flexible slot; do not skip the second revision pass.
